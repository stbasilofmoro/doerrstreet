The work itself

Six things, done
the same way.

Inspection, maintenance and compliance for industrial track — walked on foot, measured, and written up against the section of Part 213 it falls under.

  1. 01

    Track inspection

    FRA Part 213 inspection of industrial leads, sidings and spur tracks — walked on foot, measured, and written up against the section.

    • Visual and physical inspection of the running surface
    • Gauge and alignment measured, not estimated
    • Rail head wear profiling
    • Tie condition classified tie by tie
    • Ballast and drainage assessment
    • Photographic report, every finding located
    • Defect map with remediation priority

  2. 02

    Switch maintenance

    Inspection, adjustment and repair of every switch component — the part of a plant lead that fails first and stops cars soonest.

    • Switch point, stock rail and closure rail inspection
    • Throw force measured
    • Switch machine testing coordinated
    • Point-to-stock-rail fit adjusted
    • Frog and guard rail wear measured
    • Lubrication and heater inspection

  3. 03

    Rail & tie replacement

    Identification and coordinated replacement of failed rail, ties and ballast — scheduled into a window rather than forced into one.

    • Defective rail identified and marked
    • Tie plate and spike assessment
    • Tie classification to FRA standard
    • Ballast fouling assessment
    • Coordination with Class I track windows
    • Re-inspection after replacement

  4. 04

    Regulatory compliance

    Guidance through FRA audits, violation remediation and standing compliance programs — including the paperwork the carrier asks for.

    • Pre-audit assessment
    • Citation review and response
    • Written compliance programs
    • Class I liaison on your behalf
    • Waiver documentation support
    • Ongoing monitoring programs

  5. 05

    Emergency response

    Mobilization for derailments, storm damage and anything that has already stopped delivery. Call rather than write — we move off the phone.

    • Available around the clock
    • Same-day response capability
    • Derailment and storm damage assessment
    • FRA notification support
    • Track re-qualification for return to service

  6. 06

    New construction review

    Independent third-party inspection of new industrial track before the railroad is asked to accept it.

    • Milestone inspections through the build
    • Specification compliance verified
    • Gauge, surface and alignment checked
    • Switch installation tested
    • Pre-acceptance documentation package

How the work runs

Simple, thorough,
accountable.

  1. Step one

    Initial assessment

    A call, the track chart if you have one, and the reason you are asking. Most of it is settled in fifteen minutes.

  2. Step two

    Site inspection

    An FRA-certified inspector walks the track on foot and measures it. Nothing is assessed from a truck window.

  3. Step three

    Written report

    Every finding cited to its section of Part 213, located to the foot, photographed and ranked by priority. In your hands in 48 hours.

  4. Step four

    Remediation

    We stay on it through the repair and re-inspect afterwards, so the record closes properly rather than trailing off.

Who this is for
