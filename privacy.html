Privacy policy

What we hold,
and why.

Plainly stated: what this site collects, what we do with it, who else sees it, and how long inspection records are kept.

Effective May 15, 2025

Doerr Street Rail Co operates trackinspections.com. This policy explains how information is handled when you visit the site or submit a contact or quote request.

Information we collect

Our contact and quote forms collect the name, company details, email, phone, location, and project description you enter. We do not collect payment information through the site.

We also collect information automatically — IP address, browser and OS data, page visit analytics, referral sources, and device type — through cookies and analytics tools.

How we use it

We use collected information to respond to inquiries, discuss services, improve the website, analyze traffic, and meet legal requirements. We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information to third parties for their marketing purposes.

Third-party services

Google Analytics and Google Ads track visitor behavior on this site. Formspree processes our contact forms and forwards submissions to us by email. You can opt out of Google tracking using their browser add-on.

Cookies

Most browsers let you refuse or delete cookies through their settings. Doing so may limit some features of the site.

Data retention

Inspection reports and related correspondence are kept as business records for as long as they may be needed for regulatory, contractual or liability purposes. Inquiries that don't become work are retained only as long as reasonably necessary.

Security

We take reasonable measures to protect the information you share with us, though no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Children's privacy

This site is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Links to other sites

Our site may link to third-party sites. We aren't responsible for the privacy practices of sites we don't operate.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll post the revised version here with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to basil@tiedisposal.com or by calling 704 321 0802.
